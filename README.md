# 📚 StudyFlow AI

> An AI-powered personalized learning companion that helps students plan, understand, practice, and track their studies.

## 🚀 Overview

StudyFlow AI is an intelligent learning and productivity assistant designed to help students prepare effectively for examinations.

Instead of simply providing information, StudyFlow AI analyzes the student's subjects, syllabus topics, study time, exam deadline, and learning progress to provide personalized recommendations.

The system combines AI-powered learning content generation, adaptive study planning, topic recommendations, and an AI tutor into a single learning platform.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Students often struggle with:

- Deciding what topic to study next
- Creating realistic study plans
- Understanding difficult concepts
- Identifying weak topics
- Maintaining consistent study progress
- Preparing effectively before examinations

Traditional study applications mainly provide static schedules or content. They do not continuously adapt to the student's learning needs.

### 💡 Solution

StudyFlow AI provides a personalized AI-powered learning workflow:

Student
   ↓
Add Subject & Exam Details
   ↓
